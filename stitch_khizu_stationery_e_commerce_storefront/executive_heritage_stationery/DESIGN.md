## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an executive-grade e-commerce experience for a prestigious stationery brand. The visual narrative bridges the tactile discipline of fine paper goods, binding craftsmanship, and precision writing instruments with modern, frictionless digital commerce.

The aesthetic is anchored in an authoritative dark mode—harnessing midnight indigo and deep royal navy to project prestige, high security, and lasting quality. Precision geometric typography brings sharp clarity to technical product specifications (such as paper GSM, binding variants, page counts, and ruling styles), while radiant amber gold accents provide targeted focal points for commerce conversion, craftsmanship guarantees, and brand accolades. The interface evokes the quiet focus of a private archive or luxury study: structured, intentional, and undeniably premium.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immersive hierarchy tailored for low-fatigue browsing and luxurious tactile presentation:

- **Canvas & Structure (`#010C1F` & `#022452`)**: The foundational dark backdrop creates extreme optical contrast, eliminating interface noise and allowing product covers, textures, and foil-embossed details to stand out. Surface elevations layer upward from deepest navy into structured indigo containers.
- **Interactive Blue (`#0B4FC4`)**: Applied strategically for functional interactive elements, secondary actions, progress milestones, and active navigational indicators.
- **Accreditation Gold (`#F5B800`)**: Reserved exclusively for high-value transactional highlights: primary "Add to Cart" triggers, premium tier flags, rating badges, and promotional ribbon callouts.
- **Text & Contrast (`#FFFFFF` & `#E8F0FB`)**: High-legibility crisp white anchors critical titles and primary labels, supported by soft tint whites for secondary specifications, metadata, and structural outlines.

## Typography

The typographic system pairs the disciplined geometric forms of **Outfit** for editorial banners, product titles, and numbers with the balanced legibility of **Manrope** for specifications, checkout interfaces, and long-form storytelling.

- **Display & Headlines (Outfit)**: Capitalizes on confident proportions and uniform stroke weight. For e-commerce headers and hero showcases, tight letter spacing delivers architectural authority reminiscent of modern luxury publishing.
- **Body & Spec Sheets (Manrope)**: Configured with generous line heights to facilitate effortless parsing of dimensions, paper grammage, ruling configurations, and shipping policies.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Rendered in uppercase or semi-bold variants with subtle tracking expansion (`0.04em` to `0.08em`), establishing neat ledger-style utility across order summaries, breadcrumbs, and SKU tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a flexible 12-column responsive fluid grid designed around rhythmic intervals:

- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: Configured with a `3rem` canvas margin and `1.5rem` gutters. Product showcases accommodate wide asymmetric compositions—allowing large notebook mockups, spine close-ups, and interactive specification matrices to breathe comfortably.
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Margins compress to `2rem` with `1rem` gutters. Multi-column category grids transition smoothly into paired product comparisons.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: Outer margins narrow to `1rem` with `0.75rem` gutters to maximize edge-to-edge product presentation. Action drawers and product detail carousels snap cleanly without clipping critical badges.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm**: Multiples of `0.5rem` drive consistent macro-separations between inventory galleries, customer reviews, and checkout steps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids generic drop shadows in favor of a layered midnight architecture enhanced with sharp directional lighting:

- **Surface Tiers**: Base canvas rests at `#010C1F`. Elevated cards, sheets, and menus lift to primary navy `#022452`, with high-elevation dialogs resting on tinted indigo `#082F68`.
- **Ghost Borders & Inset Highlights**: Elevated containers use subtle `1px` borders (`rgba(232, 240, 251, 0.08)` on dark, stepping to `rgba(232, 240, 251, 0.16)` on hover) to define boundaries without muddying the dark canvas.
- **Ambient Product Shadows**: Stationery mockups and notebooks leverage soft, directional tinted drop shadows (`0 20px 40px -15px rgba(1, 12, 31, 0.8)`) accompanied by a subtle ambient blue glow (`0 0 25px rgba(11, 79, 196, 0.12)`), simulating gallery display lighting.
- **Modal & Drawer Overlays**: Overlays introduce a deep backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) tinted with 85% opacity `#010C1F` to maintain focus during cart preview and quick-view actions.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined **Soft** shape language (`roundedness: 1`):

- Standard components (buttons, text fields, chips, selectors) feature an intentional `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, mirroring the crisp, clean trim of freshly bound paper blocks and book edges.
- Medium containers and cards scale to `0.5rem` (8px), maintaining structured discipline without appearing overly casual or circular.
- Dialogs and primary checkout modules use `0.75rem` (12px).
- Strict circular rounding (`rounded-full`) is reserved solely for status dots, quantity steppers, and pill-style badge overlays.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Transactional ("Add to Cart / Buy Now")**: Background filled with gold accent (`#F5B800`), dark navy text (`#010C1F`), semi-bold `Manrope` font, and `0.25rem` corners. Hover state deepens slightly with a subtle warm glow (`0 0 16px rgba(245, 184, 0, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Functional**: Deep blue background (`#0B4FC4`) with crisp white typography. Hover elevates brightness with subtle translate lift.
- **Ghost & Outlined**: Transparent fill with `1px` border (`rgba(232, 240, 251, 0.2)`), white text, shifting to a solid `#022452` fill on interaction.

### Chips & Specification Tags
- **Page Count & Ruling Badges**: Compact pill tokens styled in `#022452` with contrasting light blue text (`#E8F0FB`) and `0.25rem` border radius.
- **Stock / Status Chips**: Featuring a tiny leading status circle (e.g., gold dot for low stock, emerald for in-stock, blue for pre-order).

### Product Cards
- **Architecture**: Bound within `#022452` background panels with faint `1px` border outlines.
- **Header Slot**: Integrates badge callouts (e.g., "200 PAGES", "HARDCOVER", "FOIL EMBOSSED") neatly pinned to top corners.
- **Media Frame**: Generous padding around notebook renders, emphasizing the cover texture and binding spine.
- **Footer**: Distinct split featuring product title in `Outfit`, pricing in bold white, and an integrated quick-action button.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Dark input fields (`#010C1F`) framed by subtle `1px` border (`rgba(232, 240, 251, 0.15)`).
- Focus states trigger a sharp `#0B4FC4` or gold accent border transition paired with a soft glow ring.
- Placeholder text utilizes muted `#E8F0FB` at 50% opacity.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Compact square/circular outlines matching the `0.25rem` roundedness token. Checked states activate `#0B4FC4` fill with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` checkmark indicators.

### Quantity Stepper & Cart Bar
- Unified segmented component housing minus/plus tactile triggers hugging an illuminated numerical count in `#FFFFFF`, designed for swift one-click adjustments.
